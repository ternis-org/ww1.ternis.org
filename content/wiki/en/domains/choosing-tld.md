## What actually matters

| Factor | Notes |
|--------|-------|
| Trust | `.de` wins in Germany, `.eu` signals Europe, `.com` is global default |
| First-year price | Often €1–3 promo — irrelevant, see next row |
| Renewal price | **This is the real price.** Some exotic TLDs renew at €40+/year |
| Transfer rules | Country TLDs (`.de`) have their own quirks vs gTLDs |
| SEO | No ranking bonus per TLD; exact-match keywords matter far more |

## The renewal-math rule

Always compare **5-year total cost**, not the promo sticker:

```text
promo €2 + 4 × €14 renewal = €58 over 5 years
flat €12 × 5 years        = €60 over 5 years
```

Nearly identical — so pick the TLD your audience trusts, not the cheapest cart.

## ternis.org's own spread

Sovereign projects use matching TLD families deliberately: `mail-free.eu` +
`mail-free.uk` for regional mail relays, `example-dns.com/.net/.org` for
infrastructure. One brand, multiple endings, zero confusion about ownership.

:::tip
Register the obvious typos of your main domain if you run anything
trust-sensitive (mail, login pages). It is cheaper than one phishing incident.
:::
